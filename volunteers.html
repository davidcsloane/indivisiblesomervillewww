Volunteering for
Indivisible Somerville

Who are we and how are we structured? Helping people who are interested in joining the pro-democracy movement to learn more about the chapter and how you can help fight the Trump agenda with fellow Somervillians.

Getting Started!

Join our group and receive our emails by signing up through the form below.

Sign Up
  1. Follow Indivisible Somerville on social media.
  2. Meet with someone from the volunteer cultivation team.
  3. Join a working group.

For more information, contact us at info@indivisiblesomerville.org with any questions or suggestions!

Working Groups

Our group gets work done through working groups, each with two or three people who are serving as points of contact, facilitate and support the working group participants, and coordinate with the other working groups in monthly coordinating meetings. Reach out to the point people from any of the working groups below to learn more and get started. Our current working groups are:

Campaigns

Coordinates participation in electoral campaigns in other states, such as organizing virtual phone banks or doorknocking efforts. Current areas of focus include the redistricting ballot initiative in California and the Virginia governor's race.

Communications

Writes email updates and newsletters, creates content for the website, and creates graphics and social media content to promote events and actions.

Events

Organizes events and coordinates group participation in rallies, marches, and protests. Current areas of focus are holding protest peacekeeper trainings and Community Defense Trainings.

Finance

Manages our funds and fundraising, and serves as the treasurer point of contact for the chapter.

Data / Tech

Manages the tech tools used by Indivisible Somerville (e.g., Mobilize, VAN, Every Action, etc.) and trains others on them.

Volunteer Cultivation

Recruits new volunteers, meets with people interested in Indivisible Somerville to share about the structure of the organization and support them in finding a way to plug in.
