Indivisible in Somerville

Indivisible is a grassroots movement of thousands of local activist groups with a mission to elect progressive leaders, rebuild our democracy, and defeat the Trump agenda.

Indivisible Somerville is the local chapter of Indivisible based in Somerville, MA. We are a group of volunteers focusing on electoral campaigns, trainings, and direct actions at the national stage.

Interested in becoming a volunteer/member of Indivisible Somerville? We invite you to fill out the volunteering form at the top. Check out our volunteering page for more information.
