Our Mission

Indivisible is a grassroots movement of thousands of local groups with a mission to elect progressive leaders, rebuild our democracy, and defeat the Trump agenda.

Indivisible Somerville is a local chapter relaunched in spring 2025. We are a group of volunteers working to oppose the Trump agenda through electoral campaigns and direct actions.
